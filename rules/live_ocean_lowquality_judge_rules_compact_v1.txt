Live Ocean Low Quality Single-Judge Compact Rules v1

Global
Check all 19 categories independently and in order. Use all available Ocean segments, not a reduced sample. Hit requires concrete evidence from allowed materials and the category threshold. If evidence is weak, missing, below threshold, or from forbidden material, return no_hit.
Final evidence explanation should be Chinese. Direct quotes may remain in source language.
Product images are allowed only for categories 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17, 19.
Priority: category 3 beats category 6 when there is no sustained product promotion intent and the live content is unrelated to bound products. Category 1 beats category 19 when there is sexual exposure or sexual implication.
Allowed material names use the new Start-input contract: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, comment_interaction, pearl_product_title, and pearl_product_image.

1 色情低俗低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: any segment frame or segment ASR shows listed sexualized exposure, sexual teasing, underwear/private-part outline, explicit chest/butt shaking, deliberate cleavage exposure, or clothing/product unsuitable for public display due to sexual exposure.
Exempt: ordinary cleavage without deliberate sexual action; normal bikini in beach context.

2 恶心恐怖低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: any segment frame or segment ASR shows indoor disgusting/horror content over 50% of frame or many insects, and no active cleaning.
Exempt: active cleaning or cleaning product use.

3 商品无关低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, comment_interaction, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: sustained pattern across available segments shows live core content unrelated to bound product and no valid sustained product promotion. Isolated unrelated segments are not enough.
Use bound product category/function/use-case as reference.

4 无互动低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, comment_interaction. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: most available segment ASR evidence, supported by comment_interaction when available, shows both no product promotion and no host-audience interaction. Do not use a fixed ratio threshold from the old workflow.

5 无实物商品展示低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: most available segment frame groups show no real physical display of bound product, only sticker/cutout/photo/screenshot/static image, no product, or unrecognizable partial product. Do not use a fixed ratio threshold from the old workflow.

6 商品描述不详细低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, comment_interaction, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Prerequisite: clear product promotion intent plus sustained product display/explanation/demonstration.
Hit: recurring evidence across available segment frames or segment ASR shows promotion gives no effective product info such as category/spec/function/use/price/discount/material/ingredients, or product talk is mostly off-topic/very low information.
Exempt: merely wearing/showing product with no promotion. If no promotion intent and unrelated content, use category 3 instead.

7 静态帧遮挡低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: any frame has an occlusion/interruption layer covering at least 80% of live screen and seriously blocks viewing.

8 非母语违规低质问题
Allowed: segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit only when segment ASR or visible text in segment frames contains Chinese/CJK characters used in product promotion, Chinglish, incomprehensible English, or severely broken English that prevents normal product understanding.
EU guard: normal English/local EU-language mixing is not a hit. German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, and other EU language words mixed with English are normal. German "Englisch" is a local-language word, not misspelled English. Isolated visible frame text noise, stylized labels, usernames, product names, or minor spelling mistakes are not enough unless overall promotion text becomes incomprehensible.

9 AIGC真实性低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: any segment frame, segment ASR, visible frame text, or Pearl product evidence shows AIGC-generated content used for product promotion and misleading authenticity or differing from bound physical product, such as fake AI voice/interview, fake before/after, AI size/style mismatch, digital human without real bound product.

10 AIGC商品展示低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: any segment frame, segment ASR, visible frame text, or Pearl product evidence shows rendered/generated product or use scene with exaggerated size/style/effect compared with bound real product.

11 录播回放低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: any segment frame shows taskbar/cursor not used for product pointing, black/green screen, abnormal jump, or nested screen with clear replay/loop/pre-recorded feature; or segment ASR directly mentions replay/loop playback.
Card-opening guard: for card opening, TCG, trading-card, lottery/spin, or similar live rooms, browser/desktop/OBS-style interface, wheel/spin page, card-list page, or small host camera window is not replay by itself. Host face-cam used to prove real-time opening is exempt when host appears live or ASR aligns with mouth movement. Need additional replay indicators such as replay/loop labels, playback controls, video timeline, repeated sequence, abnormal jump, black/green screen, or ASR saying replay/loop.

12 功能效果虚假宣传低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: any segment frame, segment ASR, or Pearl product evidence exaggerates product function/effect beyond reliable capability and misleads consumers, such as dramatic before/after, instant effects, fast weight loss, instant whitening, overnight body change.
Exempt: ordinary improvement, price, discount, or lottery mechanics without exaggerated product effect.

13 危险行为低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: cumulative at least 2 segment frame or segment ASR evidence units showing life-threatening violent behavior, dangerous goods demonstration, or heavy-smoke products creating fire illusion.
Exempt: normal safe product demonstration.

14 站外引流交易低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, segment_frame_visible_text, comment_interaction. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: same scene has both off-platform payment/account/site/app info and explicit transaction guidance.
Exempt: platform-internal order guidance; social account jump without purchase intent; bio link only; only one of the two core conditions.

15 违法违规商品低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: cumulative at least 2 segment frame, segment ASR, or Pearl product evidence units show legally prohibited goods, counterfeit/imitation/inferior product, product differs from bound product, or missing qualification claim tied to bound product.
Exempt: normal legal goods and ordinary product demonstration.

16 政治敏感低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: cumulative at least 2 segment frame, segment ASR, or Pearl product evidence units show political controversy, sensitive political symbols/movements, political hate symbols, racial discrimination, or polarized political narrative.

17 争议话题低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: cumulative at least 2 segment frame, segment ASR, or Pearl product evidence units show sensitive religion, racial discrimination, moral boundary, LGBT, or similarly high-conflict socio-cultural themes. Do not extend beyond this scope.

18 负面引导低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, comment_interaction. Pearl product image forbidden.
Hit: cumulative at least 2 segment frame, segment ASR, or Comment Interaction evidence units show persistent negative emotional guidance, wealth flaunting, hostile comparison, jealousy, resentment, irritability, dissatisfaction triggers, anxiety induction, or purchase-pressure emotion.
Card/lottery guard: in card opening, TCG, trading-card, lottery/spin, or similar live rooms, casual slang, short emotional reactions, disappointment about pull results, comments on card art/design, or hype words such as disgusting/crazy/insane used as slang are not a hit by themselves.
Exempt: neutral/positive content; ordinary product comparison without negative emotion triggering; ordinary card-pull disappointment; casual streamer banter; slang or hype language without sustained negative guidance; comments on card art/design or pull luck that do not induce viewer anxiety, resentment, jealousy, or purchase pressure.

19 隐私敏感商品低质问题
Allowed: segment_frame, segment_asr, pearl_product_title, pearl_product_image.
Hit: cumulative at least 2 segment frame, segment ASR, or Pearl product evidence units show intimate, gender-specific, private-use non-pornographic product tied to bound product, such as female underwear, sanitary products, nipple covers, private body-use products.
Exempt: normal shaver display in non-private context. Sexual exposure/implication belongs to category 1, not category 19.
